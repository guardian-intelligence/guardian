Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

This policy covers anveio.com, run by Anveio Foundation (“Anveio”, “we”), a Delaware corporation. That includes this website, the Anveio deck at anveio.com/deck, and anveio.com/shovon, our founder Shovon Hasan’s contact card. The card is an experimental prototype: Rumi, the assistant on it, follows a script, and nothing you leave there is sent to anyone.

That is everything. The site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and runs no analytics or advertising. Apart from Cloudflare, which carries the site, the only outside service a page talks to is Google, and only when you sign in with Google. Cloudflare asks your browser to remember, for up to a week, where to send the network error reports above; the browser keeps that instruction itself, and you can clear it with your browsing data.

Sign in with Google

On anveio.com/shovon you can sign in with Google so Rumi knows who is leaving a message. We ask Google only for your basic profile: your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture. Google sends these to your browser, never to our servers. The page checks them against Google’s published keys, holds them while it is open and forgets them when you close or reload it. We don’t receive your password or access to anything else in your Google account.

We use information received from Google APIs only to sign you in. Our use adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove anveio.com from your Google account at any time in your Google Account settings.

Messages to Rumi

Rumi is a prototype. Today she doesn’t use your microphone, and nothing you say or type on anveio.com/shovon is recorded, sent or stored. Before she starts taking real voice or text messages, we will update this policy to say what is kept, where and for how long.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you by writing to shovon@anveio.com.

Children

This site isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes
